## What to build

For every ingredient in `data/ingredients/`, add September to `freshMonths` and,
where relevant, `storageMonths`, sourced from satokausi.fi. This applies broadly,
not only to the two ingredients that already had a September entry (bok choy,
suppilovahvero), since Tia's own data only ever covered January through April and
says nothing about whether carrots, cabbage, or potatoes are in season in
September, even though they plausibly are. Every month added this way is
`verified: false`.

**Sourcing note:** satokausi.fi has an individual page per ingredient at
`satokausi.fi/raaka-aineet/<finnish-name>/`, each with a real month breakdown into
storage season, in season, and peak season (Finnish: Varastosesonki, Sesongissa,
Huippusesonki), which maps directly onto `storageMonths`, `freshMonths`, and
`peakMonths`. Confirmed working on `porkkana` (carrot) already. Fetch each
ingredient's page directly and use what it actually says, not general knowledge.

This slice should also correct existing months, not only add September. Right
now every ingredient's January-to-April data was imported as a flat list from
Notion's "Season" column with no fresh-versus-storage distinction, so it all
landed in `freshMonths`. Carrot's real page says January to April is storage
season, not fresh, so that split needs fixing here too, for every ingredient this
touches, not just carrot.

## Done

Sourced in this session: `WebFetch` reaches satokausi.fi, so the Cowork detour
this issue describes was not needed. 73 seasonal ingredients, one page each.

- 43 fresh in September, 3 from Finnish storage, 3 worth buying imported,
  21 not in season, 3 with no satokausi page. Listed in
  `docs/SEPTEMBER-REVIEW.md`, regenerated by `npm run review:september`.

  an ingredient whose January is trusted. 48 ingredients changed.

  `docs/SATOKAUSI-CONFLICTS.md` and deliberately not acted on.
- `scripts/september-source.mjs` holds the transcription of what each page said,
  and `scripts/apply-september.mjs` is the one-shot that applied it.

Reviewed with Tia, five judgment calls settled:

- Button mushroom and oyster mushroom, cultivated year-round, count as fresh.
- Cauliflower really is from storage in September, and beetroot and turnip
  really are not in season. satokausi's readings stand.
- Bok choy keeps Tia's verified September and gains satokausi's peak marking.
- Basil, coriander, and thyme stay empty until step 3 finds a source.
